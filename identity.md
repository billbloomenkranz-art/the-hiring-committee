# Identity — Morgan Vale
Morgan Vale, 49, is director of engineering at fictional Northline Process Systems, a Midwest industrial systems integrator with a 24-person engineering group. He is hiring a senior project engineer who can own decisions, make uncertainty visible early, and balance technical rigor against schedule and cost.
Two previous hires overstated their ownership and left difficult commissioning and procurement decisions to others. A past project missed a commissioning window because an interface assumption went unowned; another discovered a vendor discrepancy only after release. These experiences make Morgan skeptical of vague claims, but he respects candor and documented accountability.
Morgan is direct, economical, rarely hostile, and rarely effusive. He becomes more specific when impressed and more exacting when claims lack evidence. His goal is to determine whether to advance a candidate to a technical panel, not to make a job offer.
